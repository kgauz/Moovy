About us

Imagine this: It’s your moving day, and instead of the excitement of a fresh start, you are greeted with frustration. The movers are late, have no materials to pack your belongings, and handle your furniture so carelessly that you’re left standing in shock, not knowing what to do. To top it all off, you’re handed a bill with unexpected extra charges that were never disclosed.

This was us a couple of years ago — we hired movers for our move and encountered a service that we wish had not happened to us. Except for one thing — our terrible experience sparked an idea: to establish a reliable moving company focused on a professional approach, clear communication, transparent pricing, and above all, satisfied clients with peace of mind.

As spring 2024 was coming into full swing, we decided to get moving ourselves! We poured our hearts into establishing our new venture, carefully training our team, and fine-tuning every process. Each piece of the puzzle was thoughtfully assembled to introduce Moovy to the world as a brand that truly cares.

We see moving as more than just transporting items. Each box holds a tapestry of your life — whether it’s a cherished mug gifted by a thoughtful colleague, home decor items brought from overseas, or a treasured collection of vinyl records inherited from a beloved grandparent. These are all irreplaceable memories that we handle with the utmost care, as if they were our own.

Our founders know that moving can be overwhelming, and our mission is to transform that experience from stressful to serene. We’ve reimagined every step of the moving process — from the first call to the final box — lifting the burden from your shoulders and celebrating the end of your move with a well-deserved glass of champagne!

We live and breathe exceptional moving care. At Moovy we offer a moving service that covers all aspects for you. We start with personalized consultation and assessing your needs, continuing with expert packing and careful disassembly of the furniture, white-glove handling of your belongings, and safe transportation to your new home, ensuring all boxes arrive in pristine condition. Our customer success representatives oversee every step to make your experience smooth and flawless.

Located in Toronto, Ontario, we offer moving services throughout the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). Whether you are moving from North York to Richmond Hill or from Toronto to Burlington, we’ve got you covered. Our fast and efficient trucks reach every neighbourhood in the GTA, from the bustling Downtown core to the quiet lakeside cottages.
