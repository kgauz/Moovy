pricing

Here at Moovy, we promise service excellence with no tricky pricing. Therefore, the complete structure is contained below.

  • Hourly Rate: A very competitive hourly rate of ours already covers the most important services for the move: packing, disassembly, and assembly. It’s this rate we apply to the whole team, so you’ll get a well-thought-out plan for the moving process.‍
  • Truck Fee: Given the travel distance from your location to our storage facility, we have applied a truck fee that, in addition to the hourly fee, goes into maintaining our reliable services by ensuring trucks are always on the road in good condition, so the people assigned to make the move don’t worry about the bad impression caused by breakdowns.
  • Minimum Charge: All moves are charged a minimum of three hours of work time plus the truck fee, to ensure that you are not denied by any means the quality via human resources that would be worthy and good enough for your relocation

Our goal is to make your moving experience as simple as possible and easy on you. So contact us today to get a personal estimate, or let us know about your specific moving needs, and we will help you with anything you need!
