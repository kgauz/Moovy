Commercial moving services: New premises, less hassle


Has the time come to relocate your business? Whether you’re moving to a new office or a more spacious warehouse, the idea of packing everything up and transporting it all can feel like a mammoth task. It’s a planning, handling, and logistical puzzle wrapped in frustration. But the good news is that commercial relocation services provided by Moovy are a boon.


No more scrambling to find packing materials or worrying about fragile equipment. No more trying to accommodate the schedule of movers at the cost of your uptime, even if you are not okay with it. We’ll do it professionally and quickly – in your way.

Your office movers in Toronto

There’s something special about office environments. This is where the backbone of a business is shaped, filling routine operations with a team spirit. When hired for business moving services, we go above and beyond to preserve this spirit while getting you to your sprawling premises.

The process starts with a consultation to understand what your commercial space is, identify how much stuff it is, and plan the logistics. On the moving day, our crew will come to carry everything you’ve packed up or provide packing assistance. We can handle:

  • Computers and printers
  • Desks, chairs, conference tables, and workstations
  • Office decor and stationery
  • Kitchen supplies and miscellaneous items

In other words, it’s everything your office has. Our commercial movers in Toronto can deal with small and large equipment and provide special-purpose packing for sensitive items like servers and document archives. We can then transport and unload them anywhere in the city or across Ontario.

Your industrial movers in Toronto

Industrial relocations are probably the hardest to pull off, as moving warehouses or factories involves an array of specialized tasks. Heavy machinery, large-scale inventories, and intricate setups all require expert handling and trucks that can accommodate them.

Moovy brings together professionally trained, insured crews and up to 26ft trucks for industrial moves. We have loading tools and equipment to transport everything your space is filled with, including:

  • Assembly line components
  • Stacks of raw materials and parts
  • Shelving units and racks
  • Cooling and heating systems

We can handle much more. The only items that are a no-go during an industrial relocation are hazardous materials that may undermine the well-being of your employees or our movers. If you have such items, check with the local authorities for risk-free disposal or rearrangement.

You say it – We’re on it

As a commercial moving company, we know you’re making arrangements to safeguard business continuity. We can help you with that by offering:

  • Flexible scheduling, including weekend commercial moving services in Toronto
  • Quick relocations within Ontario
  • Hourly pricing

With us, you don’t have to disrupt your business operations during weekdays. Our crew will be there for you whenever it works for your company. We can send multiple movers, packers, and trucks for a single relocation.

You can budget for an industrial or corporate moving service by requesting a quote from Moovy.
