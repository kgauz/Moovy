How do I get a quote for my move?

−

You can easily get a quote by filling out our online inquiry form with details of your move, including your current and destination addresses, preferred move date, and our specialist will call you back within 20 minutes to gain more information.

Alternatively, you can contact us directly via phone or email.

How long will my move take?

+

The duration depends on the size of your home and the distance between locations.

  • Studio: 2.5 – 3.5 hours
  • Bedroom Apartment: 4.0 – 5.0 hours
  • Bedroom Apartment/House: 5.0 – 7.0 hours
  • Bedroom Apartment/House: 8.5 – 10.0 hours

Additional factors such as packing, furniture assembly, elevator reservations, and preparation can affect the overall duration.

How far in advance should I hire movers?

+

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially during peak moving season in the summer.

What's your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

+

We do not require deposits and there are no cancellation or rescheduling fees. Please let us know as early as possible if your plans change.

How is the cost of a move calculated?

+

The total cost is based on the hourly rate, estimated move duration, truck fee, and applicable taxes.

Example:

  • Hourly Rate: $169/hour
  • Estimated Duration: 6 hours
  • Truck Fee: $100
  • Tax: 13%

Estimated Total: $1,258.82

Can you pack everything for us?

+

Yes. We can handle the entire packing process. Packing is included within the hourly service time and does not carry a separate service charge.

Can you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

+

Yes. Our team can dismantle and reassemble furniture as part of the moving service without additional service fees.

What items are movers not allowed to transport?

+

Hazardous materials such as propane tanks, flammable liquids, explosives, and toxic substances cannot be transported for safety reasons.

What happens if something is damaged during the move?

+

We take every precaution to avoid damages. In the unlikely event that damage occurs, coverage options are available for repair or compensation.

How does Moovy manage the relocation of heavy items such as pianos?

+

Heavy items such as pianos require specialized equipment and trained movers. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare properly and provide an accurate quote.
